What we work on

Phonics and early reading (ages 4–6)

Letter sounds, blending and segmenting, reading fluency and first sentences. A secure start in phonics makes every later stage of English easier, so I take the time to get it right.

KS1 and KS2 English (ages 5–11)

Reading comprehension, vocabulary, spelling, grammar and punctuation, plus creative and non-fiction writing. Lessons follow what your child is learning at school and fill any gaps along the way.

KS2 SATs English

Preparation for the Year 6 reading paper and the grammar, punctuation and spelling tests: the question types, how to manage the time, and how to check answers.

11+ and school entrance English

Comprehension, inference, vocabulary and creative writing for 7+, 8+ and 11+ papers at grammar and independent schools. More about 11+ preparation.

GCSE English Language and Literature

Analysing unseen extracts, revising set texts, planning and structuring essays, and timed writing practice, with a clear focus on what the mark scheme rewards. More about GCSE tuition.

How lessons work

We start with a short phone call about your child, their school and what you'd like to improve. Every lesson after that is planned around your child: what they're covering at school, where they're getting stuck, and what they need next. I'm a PGCE-qualified teacher with Qualified Teacher Status, over 10 years' UK classroom experience and an Enhanced DBS check.
