## Platform

## Stack

Astro static site (from `tools/preview-starter`). The current live site is plain HTML/CSS on GitHub Pages (https://magganesh.github.io/Mits-Website/); this preview is a redesign candidate, not yet the live site.

## Users

Parents in Stanmore and the London Borough of Harrow looking for a private tutor, usually on a phone after a school report, a worry about reading or maths, or with the 11+ approaching. **Primary focus (confirmed by Mags, 15 Nov 2026): parents of 4–11 year olds** — phonics and early reading, KS1/KS2, SATs and 11+ preparation. Secondary: KS3 and GCSE (English, Maths).

They arrive from Google ("tutor near me", "english tutor stanmore", "11 plus tutor harrow") or a Nextdoor recommendation, and want to decide quickly whether she is qualified, trustworthy, near enough and affordable, then call or WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Future Foundations Tutoring is Mithusha's one-to-one tutoring practice. The site's job is to turn a local parent's search into a call or WhatsApp message. Success = enquiries by phone/WhatsApp.

## Positioning

A qualified UK classroom teacher (PGCE with QTS, 10+ years, former primary school teacher) tutoring one-to-one, not a tuition centre or a student tutor. Lessons at her home in Stanmore Place, at the family's home anywhere in Harrow, or online. Local parents recommend her on Nextdoor.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: qualifications, DBS and real parent words arrive before any sales push.
2. The phone is the funnel: call/WhatsApp reachable from every viewport on mobile.
3. Lead with young children and 11+, without hiding GCSE.
4. Local and personal, not corporate: one teacher, real neighbourhoods.
5. Prices in plain sight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Parents of all ages on mid-range phones: WCAG AA contrast, large tap targets, readable body size, reduced-motion respected.
