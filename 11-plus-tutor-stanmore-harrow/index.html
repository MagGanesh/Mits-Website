What we cover

11+ English

Reading comprehension and inference, vocabulary, spelling, grammar and punctuation, and creative writing to a time limit.

11+ Maths

Arithmetic, multi-step problem solving and reasoning, including topics that go beyond the Year 5 school curriculum, practised for speed as well as accuracy.

Exam technique

Timed practice papers, pacing, checking answers and staying calm on the day. Children who know the format do better than those meeting it for the first time.

I teach the Maths and English papers. If the schools you're applying to also test verbal or non-verbal reasoning, tell me when we first speak and we'll talk about how to cover it.

When to start

Most grammar school 11+ exams are sat in September of Year 6, so many families start preparing in Year 4 or Year 5. Starting earlier means steady weekly lessons rather than a rush at the end. If your child's exam is sooner, call me and we'll work out a realistic plan.

Why parents choose me

I'm a PGCE-qualified teacher with Qualified Teacher Status, over 10 years' UK classroom experience and an Enhanced DBS check. Every 11+ plan is built for your child, not taken from a template.
