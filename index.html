Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do you tutor English?

Yes. I teach phonics and early reading, KS1 and KS2 English (reading comprehension, spelling, grammar, punctuation and creative writing), SATs, the English papers for 4+, 7+, 8+ and 11+ entrance exams, and GCSE English.

Which subjects and levels do you teach?

Phonics and early years, KS1 and KS2 English, Maths and Science (including SATs), KS3 Maths and Science, GCSE English, Maths and Biology, and 4+, 7+, 8+ and 11+ entrance preparation in Maths and English.

Where do lessons take place?

At my home in Stanmore Place, Stanmore, at your home anywhere in the London Borough of Harrow (including Harrow, Wealdstone, Harrow Weald, Kenton, Pinner, Hatch End, Edgware and Canons Park), or online anywhere in the UK.

How much do lessons cost?

Lessons at my home in Stanmore and online lessons are £40 per hour. Home visits anywhere in the London Borough of Harrow are from £60 per hour.

What qualifications do you have?

I'm a qualified UK teacher with a PGCE and Qualified Teacher Status (QTS), a BSc in Medical Genetics from Queen Mary University of London, over 10 years' classroom experience, and an Enhanced DBS check.

When are you available?

Evenings and weekends, during term time and school holidays.

How do we get started?

Call or WhatsApp me on 07518 327252 for a no-obligation chat. I'll ask about your child, their school and their goals, then suggest a plan.
