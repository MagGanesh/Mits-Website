Do you tutor English?

Yes. I teach phonics and early reading, KS1 and KS2 English (reading comprehension, spelling, grammar, punctuation and creative writing), and the English papers for 4+, 7+, 8+ and 11+ entrance exams.

Which subjects and levels do you teach?

Phonics and early years, KS1 and KS2 English, Maths and Science, KS3 Maths and Science, GCSE Maths and GCSE Biology, and 4+, 7+, 8+ and 11+ school entrance preparation.

Where do lessons take place?

In person in Stanmore and nearby areas including Harrow, Canons Park, Harrow Weald and Edgware, or online anywhere in the UK.

I'm a qualified UK teacher with a PGCE and Qualified Teacher Status (QTS), a BSc in Medical Genetics from Queen Mary University of London, over 10 years' classroom experience, and an Enhanced DBS check.

Evenings and weekends, during term time and school holidays.
