What we work on

KS1 and KS2 Maths (ages 5–11)

Number sense, place value, times tables, the four operations, fractions, measurement and problem solving. Gaps in these basics cause most later difficulties in Maths, so we find them and fix them.

KS2 SATs Maths

Preparation for the Year 6 arithmetic and reasoning papers: fast, accurate written methods, reading multi-step questions carefully, and showing working to pick up marks.

11+ and school entrance Maths

Problem solving, speed and accuracy for 7+, 8+ and 11+ Maths papers at grammar and independent schools. More about 11+ preparation.

KS3 Maths (ages 11–14)

The move from primary to secondary Maths: algebra, ratio and proportion, negative numbers and geometry, cemented before GCSE study begins.

GCSE Maths

Working through the topics your child finds hardest, past-paper practice and exam technique, so they walk into the exam knowing what to expect. More about GCSE tuition.

How lessons work

We start with a short phone call about your child, their school and where they're finding Maths hard. Lessons then follow a plan built around your child, with clear explanations, plenty of practice and regular checks that new topics have stuck. I'm a PGCE-qualified teacher with Qualified Teacher Status, over 10 years' UK classroom experience and an Enhanced DBS check.
